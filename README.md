# UI Measure

Chrome extension (MV3, WXT + React) that measures element size and spacing on any page, Figma Alt-hover style. Vocabulary: [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md). Background research: `~/Downloads/figma-measure-extension-research.md`.

## Load in Chrome

`chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → Load unpacked → pick `ui-measure/.output/chrome-mv3` (or `chrome-mv3-dev` while `pnpm dev` runs). `.output` is hidden in the macOS picker: press ⌘⇧. to show hidden folders, or ⌘⇧G and paste the full path. Use the prod build for the manual checklist: the dev build adds `tabs` + `localhost` host permissions, which hides `activeTab` bugs.

## Use

- Toggle: toolbar icon or `Ctrl+Shift+E` (`⌘⇧E` on a Mac; rebind at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`, the icon title names the bound one). Badge `ON` per tab, `!` when Chrome forbids the page (chrome://, Web Store).
- Hint: a dark pill 16px below-right of the pointer names the next keys: `Click` to select; with a selection `⌥ Option` measure (`Alt` measure off a Mac) · `C` copy · `B` children · `I` styles · `S` stacking, plus `Click` again → parent where a click would climb; in Blueprint `B` / `Esc` to exit · `I` styles; in Stacking `S` / `Esc` to exit · `I` styles, plus `Click` again → below where a click would go down; with Styles open `I` / `Esc` to close. Flips at the right / bottom edge, hidden while measuring and once the pointer rests 0.8s (back on the next move, click or key). Labels move out of its way; a padding / gap / radius value under it is hidden until the pointer rests, never moved onto a leader.
- Hover: outline, W × H, padding (green) and margin (orange) bands, no other numbers. Moving to another element, that outline and everything on it glide together: bands, W × H, and for one selected element its padding, radius, gaps and the measurement lines. The numbers stay the final values, and Blueprint boxes stay put. No motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Labels are rounded with a soft shadow; size is blue, measurement red, gap purple, padding green.
- Click: select the deepest box under the pointer. Elements sharing its bounds (±0.5px, each the only child of the next) are one box, as in Blueprint: the inner one with padding or border, or a rounded wrapper over a plain child, with a wrapper's radius when the inner one has none (a padded row inside a same-size rounded card shows both on the first click). The hover outline is that same box. Click the one selected element again for the next box up, past its same-size wrappers, and the same spot again to keep climbing (stays at the top, `<body>` when it has `<html>`'s bounds; a double-click is two clicks, so it lands one box up); click another element to go back down. Page clicks are blocked while on. One selected element shows its details with no key: W × H, hatched padding with values, corner radius arcs with the clamped value (`9999px` pill → `r 16`; inside its corner only when the box is at least 68px tall, so a line of its text stays readable; on a shorter control it sits just above or below the corner on a leader from the arc. A W × H that cannot sit beside a short control without covering its words steps out on a leader too), gaps of a flex / grid / block container; margins stay a band. `Shift`+click adds / removes an element (picked by the same rule); measurements then start from the union of the selection (dashed box, one W × H, no details). Details are hidden while measuring. A padding / gap / radius value with no free spot next to what it describes sits 16 or 32px away on a leader: a dashed line in its color from a dot on its band, gap line or arc to the label's nearest edge (a padding value first slides along its band, a radius tries inside its corner first, then just above or below that corner, then out along the 45° line). A leader is at most 40px and crosses no label; a value with no such spot is hidden (hover the box to bring back one covered by another box's label; a gap comes back with either box beside it too). The selected element's own values take their spots and leaders before any Blueprint descendant's labels. A radius value that does not fit inside its box, and a W × H wider than its box, are not drawn on top of the icon: the radius goes to its leader, the size steps out to the side.
- Hold `Alt` (`⌥ Option` on a Mac) and hover another element: spacing / inset / offset to the selection, dashed guides when diagonal. A value too short for its label sits beside its line instead of on it; a line under 6px gets outward arrows (`→| |←`).
- Small distances: numbers have up to 2 decimals; a W × H under 0.01 reads `<0.01`. Edges half a device pixel or less apart (counted in CSS px inside CSS `zoom`) are touching: that spacing / inset / offset / gap / padding is not drawn at all (no `0`). So it depends on the display: a 0.3px spacing is hidden at DPR 1 and shown on a Retina (DPR 2) screen, and browser zoom changes it too. Copy keeps the real values.
- Gap (purple): when the one selected element is a flex / grid / block container, the empty space between consecutive children, measured between their bounds (margins and `justify-content` space included, absolute / fixed children ignored). A block container's children are grouped into lines like a grid: stacked blocks give vertical gaps, inline-blocks on one line horizontal ones (the ~4px whitespace between them included); one with direct text (a paragraph) gets none, since the line would cross the text. Like a measurement, a gap too short for its label has the value beside its line, not on it. A click usually lands on a child: click it again for the container. Hidden while measuring.
- `B`: Blueprint adds the details of the last-selected element's descendants: dashed outline and W × H for each descendant in view (a descendant holding two or more drawn boxes, like a tab list, shows its W × H only when hovered: centered, it would sit under its middle tab), hatched padding (green, with values), gaps (flex / grid / block containers), corner radius arcs, values without room on leaders. Wrappers the size of their only child count once; more than 8 levels or 400 boxes show `+N hidden`. Labels that don't fit are hidden (a descendant's W × H takes no leader, so the details values keep the room); hover a box to bring its labels back. `B` or `Esc` returns to the selection. Ignored while typing; hidden while measuring.
- `C` with a selection: copy the last-selected element's spec (selector, border-box size, padding, border, radius) as CSS; toast `Copied CSS` or why it failed. A toast shows at once, stays 1.5s, then fades out over 200ms (reduced motion too: it doesn't move), keeping its spot until gone; `C` again shows the new one at full. Works on http pages too (copy command, no extra permission). Ignored while typing; Cmd/Ctrl+C stay with the page.
- `I` with a selection: Styles, a dark panel of the last-selected element's computed CSS that the overlay does not draw. Rows `name  value` in groups: display and layout, the font and color, fill, border (width per side as a shorthand, `0 0 2px`, then style and color), shadows and effects, margin (the one place a margin has numbers). Display, the font and color always (`font-size` and `line-height` each on its own row), the rest only where it differs from a plain `<div>`. `z-index  5 · no effect (static)` (or `(svg)`) where z-index does nothing, though the computed value still reads 5. Colors are hex with the opacity below 100% (`#6366F1 50%`) and a swatch, each shadow one row (`0 4 12 0 #000000 25%`), the name only on the first, long values cut with `…`. Sits 8px below the selection, else above, else beside, else in the bottom-right corner, always in the viewport; it never follows the hover. Labels move out of its way like the Hint's. Display-only (no click, no scroll). Hidden while measuring, back on Alt release; shown alongside Blueprint; `I` or `Esc` closes it, clearing the selection too. Ignored while typing.
- `S` with a selection: Stacking, for the last-selected element: why its z-index wins or loses. Its label says its z-index (`z 9999`, `z auto` when positioned), `· no effect (static)` (or `(svg)`) where z-index does nothing, the reasons when it is a stacking context itself (`z 0 · transform`), or `top layer (dialog)`. Its stacking context, the nearest flat-tree ancestor that is one (through closed shadow roots too), gets a solid pink outline and `div.card · transform, will-change · z 0`: every reason, since a trap only goes once all are removed, and the z it competes with; none when that is `<html>` or the selection is in the top layer. The nearest ancestor that cuts its painting (`overflow` other than visible, `clip-path`, `contain: paint` / `content-visibility`) among those its bounds run past (one it fits inside cuts nothing off), gets a dashed outline and `clipped by main · overflow hidden`: no z-index fixes that. An absolute / fixed box escapes the overflow of boxes below its containing block, and overflow on `<html>` / `<body>` goes to the viewport, so those aren't reported. Each part shows only when it differs from the plain case. Ancestors are cut to the viewport, their labels inside the part on screen. The selection keeps its outline and W × H, without padding / gap / radius values. Click the selection again: the next box *below* it at that point (`elementsFromPoint`, past its same-size wrappers), so a modal under a translucent backdrop is reachable; again to keep going down, to `<html>`, where it stays; a click where the selection isn't selects the topmost box. With several selected, Stacking describes the last one and a click never goes below. Same-size wrappers stay one box below too, unless something else paints between them (a `z -1` box between `<body>` and `<html>`): then the inner one is picked, so that box is reachable. The selection's label shows its declared z-index (`z auto`), its context's the one it competes with (`z 0` for auto). Pointer-events: none and inert content can't be reached. `S` / `B` switch between Stacking and Blueprint; Styles can be open with it; hidden while measuring; gone with the selection. Ignored while typing. Each frame answers for its own document (an iframe is one box).
- `Esc`: close Styles, then leave Stacking or Blueprint, then clear selection, then turn off.
- Every other key (`Tab`, `Enter`, other letters) goes to the page.
- Iframes: turning on reaches the top frame and every same-origin / srcdoc frame; each frame draws its own overlay with its own selection (no measuring between frames). Keys go to the frame you last clicked; Esc there clears its selection, then turns every frame off. Cross-origin frames need access: a toast names their hosts, right-click the icon → **Measure cross-origin frames** and allow in Chrome's prompt (asks for those hosts only, remembered by Chrome). Frames added after turning on join at the next turn-on.

## Layout

```
src/core/       pure geometry (rect, measure, gap, labels, hint, blueprint, motion), stacking rules, Copy + Styles formatters (spec, styles) + DOM reads (dom.ts); no React, no chrome.*
src/overlay/    controller.ts (input + per-frame re-read), Overlay.tsx (SVG), theme.ts (colors, strokes, font by role), clipboard.ts
src/entrypoints background.ts (toggle, badge), measure.content.tsx (shadow root + top layer)
playground/     fixture page for manual testing (also served by e2e)
e2e/smoke.mjs   pnpm e2e
public/icon/    toolbar + store icons, rendered from docs/icon.svg (16/32/48 as is, 128 with viewBox -1 -1 18 18 for store padding)
```

## Manual checklist (on your Chrome: what `pnpm e2e` can't do headless, i.e. the real icon and shortcut, Alt+Tab, Chrome's permission prompt, real pages and the look)

1. Side by side / stacked / diagonal / overlap / nested card on the playground show the expected numbers.
2. Button and link do nothing while ON; work again after OFF.
3. Hold Alt, Alt+Tab away, come back: no stuck measuring chip.
4. Select an element, focus the input, press Alt / letters (`B` and `C` too) / Enter / Tab: typing and focus move still work, Blueprint and selection do not change; `Esc` still exits.
5. Select inside the scroll container, scroll: outline follows. Sticky header measures correctly.
6. Iframes: turn ON on the playground: the srcdoc and same-origin frames hover / select, the toast names `127.0.0.1` (or `localhost`); right-click the icon → Measure cross-origin frames, allow: the third frame measures too. Esc inside a frame, then again: every frame turns off.
7. Top layer: open the dialog, then turn ON: overlay draws above it. Then click "Open dialog in 2 s" (and "Open popover in 2 s") and turn ON right away: when it opens, the overlay still draws above it.
8. `chrome://extensions` tab: badge shows `!`. Navigate a tab that was ON: badge clears.
9. `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`: `Ctrl+Shift+E` (`⌘⇧E` on a Mac) is bound (another extension may hold it; a suggested key only applies on install, so remove and re-add the unpacked extension after changing it), and the icon title names it; clear it there: the title names none.
10. Blue icon (not a letter) in the toolbar, extensions menu and `chrome://extensions`, readable in light and dark theme; red `ON` badge sits on it.
11. Select an element, press `C` and paste into an editor, on an https page and a plain-http one: CSS with a selector that finds the element in DevTools.
12. Click a tile of the playground's row 3 or 4 times at the same spot: the selection climbs tile → row → section → …; click another tile: back to it.
13. Small distances (last playground block), on a Retina display (DPR 2): select each pair's first tile and Alt-hover the second: 1 and 0.5 are drawn and labelled beside the line with outward arrows, 0.25 and 0.05 draw nothing. Select the padded box: padding 1 top / bottom only. In the `zoom: 3` row, 1 is labelled `1`, 0.1 draws nothing. On a DPR 1 screen 0.5 draws nothing too.
14. Leaders: a list row with side padding and a radius (the playground's `#leader-row`, then a real one, e.g. a settings list): one click shows `r 12` (or the row's radius) and both side paddings, none hidden. If the radius sits on a same-size parent, the first click selects that parent. Select `#leader-dense`, press `B`: values without room hang on short dashed leaders in their color, none crossing a label; move the pointer around: no value jumps back and forth.
15. Block gaps on real text-heavy pages (a news article, a dashboard): select the article / a card and press `B`. Gap lines between blocks, none across a paragraph's text, and the purple labels don't flood the page (if they do, lower gaps below padding: ticket 20, "Block gaps").
16. Windows with Unikey or EVKey switching V/E on `Ctrl+Shift`: `Ctrl+Shift+E` toggles measure mode without switching the input mode.
17. Styles on a real page (a card, a button): select it, press `I`. The panel's colors, font and shadow match DevTools → Computed, the swatches show the colors; scroll so the element nears the bottom, then fills the viewport: the panel moves above, then to a corner, never off-screen. Press `B`: both show; `Esc` closes Styles first.
18. Toast fade on the prod build, light and dark pages: press `C`. After 1.5s `Copied CSS` fades out with its shadow (no grey smear left behind), quick at first, gone in about 0.2s; press `C` while it fades: back at full at once. If the Hint pushes it from the bottom to the top mid-fade and that reads as a jump, keep its spot while fading (ticket 24, open risk).
19. Crowded details on a real page (a toolbar of icon buttons, a tab bar): select the toolbar, press `B`: every gap value shows; rest the pointer on an icon: the Hint goes, the gaps beside it show. Select the tab bar's parent, press `B`: each tab's W × H sits by that tab, the list's only on hover.
20. Stacking on a real page with a "my z-index doesn't work" case (a dropdown under a sticky header, a modal behind a backdrop, a tooltip in a card with `overflow: hidden`): select it, press `S`. The label and the outlined stacking context match what DevTools → Computed shows (every reason listed); a clipped one names the clipping ancestor; with a backdrop over a modal, click the backdrop again: the modal. Compare a few with Edge DevTools → 3D View → Z-index if at hand.
